### Information we may process
Depending on whether you order, provide food, deliver orders or administer the service, QuickEats may process:
- account and contact details, such as name, phone number and email address;
- order details, items, quantities, prices, delivery instructions and order history;
- delivery location and location information needed to match, navigate and complete an active delivery;
- vendor business, menu, payout and order-fulfilment details;
- rider profile, vehicle and identity/verification documents where required for rider onboarding and safety;
- payment references and payment status supplied by payment providers. QuickEats should not collect or store full card numbers, PINs or CVVs;
- messages and support/complaint records;
- technical, security and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

QuickEats should not collect information that is not needed for a stated business or legal purpose.

### Why information is used
Information may be used to create and manage accounts, match orders to vendors, calculate and communicate prices and delivery fees, process payments through payment providers, dispatch riders, share necessary delivery details, provide customer support, prevent fraud, maintain records, secure the platform and meet legal obligations.

### Legal grounds
For each processing activity, QuickEats must identify and document an applicable lawful basis under Nigerian law, such as taking steps requested before a contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, protecting vital interests, carrying out a properly assessed legitimate interest, or consent where consent is the appropriate basis. Consent must not be used as a blanket substitute for identifying the correct lawful basis.

### Who may receive information
Information should be shared only where needed with:
- the selected vendor, for preparing and fulfilling the order;
- the assigned rider, for pickup and delivery;
- payment providers, to process and verify payment;
- hosting, database, messaging, mapping and security providers supporting the service;
- regulators, law-enforcement bodies or other parties where disclosure is legally required.

QuickEats must document its service providers, assess their safeguards and put appropriate written data-processing terms in place. Do not publish a list of providers until it has been verified.

### Location information
Precise location should be collected only when necessary for an active delivery or a clearly explained feature. Explain when location is used, who can see it and when collection stops. Do not continuously track riders or customers when the feature does not require it.

### Retention
Keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including applicable accounting, dispute, safety and legal recordkeeping requirements. Set and document retention periods for orders, payment records, support records, rider verification documents, location data, logs and inactive accounts. Delete or securely anonymise information when it is no longer needed, unless law requires retention.

### Your rights
Subject to applicable law and exceptions, individuals may have rights to be informed, access their information, correct inaccurate information, request erasure, restrict or object to certain processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

**How to make a request:** Contact [ADD OFFICIAL SUPPORT EMAIL/PHONE]. QuickEats must verify the requester proportionately, record the request, respond within the applicable legal timeframe and explain any lawful reason why information cannot be erased immediately (for example, a legal record-retention duty).

### Security and incidents
QuickEats should use role-based access, least privilege, strong authentication for administrators, private storage for identity documents, secure transport, secret management, backups, audit logging and timely patching. Access to rider identity documents and customer addresses must be limited to people who need them. Suspected data incidents must be escalated immediately, investigated, contained and assessed for notification duties under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and applicable NDPC guidance.

### Children
QuickEats is not designed for independent use by children. Do not knowingly create child accounts or collect children's personal information without an approved lawful process and any required parent/guardian authorisation.

### Changes
Material changes to this notice should be communicated before or when they take effect, as required by law.

## 2. Customer terms of service — draft

1. **Service.** QuickEats provides a platform for customers to request food from participating vendors and arrange delivery through participating riders. Availability depends on participating vendors, riders, location, operating hours and technical availability.
2. **Orders.** Review the selected items, quantities, vendor, delivery address, instructions, fees and total before confirming. An order is not accepted until the platform confirms it under the displayed order flow.
3. **Prices and fees.** Item prices are supplied by vendors or displayed menu data. Delivery fees and any other charge must be shown before payment. No fee should be added after confirmation without the customer's agreement, except where a clearly disclosed lawful adjustment applies.
4. **Payment.** Payments are processed by the payment provider shown at checkout. QuickEats should confirm payment using a verified provider response, not a screenshot or customer assertion. Never send a card PIN or CVV to QuickEats or a vendor.
5. **Availability and substitutions.** If an item becomes unavailable or the vendor cannot fulfil it, the customer should be told and offered an appropriate choice, including removal/refund where applicable. Do not substitute items or increase the price without agreement.
6. **Delivery.** Customers must provide a reasonably accurate address and useful delivery instructions. Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees, unless expressly stated otherwise.
7. **Cancellations, refunds and complaints.** The draft policy below applies, subject to mandatory consumer rights and applicable law.
8. **Fair use.** Users must not misuse the platform, submit fraudulent orders, harass workers or interfere with platform security.
9. **Suspension.** QuickEats may restrict an account where reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, protect users or comply with law. Provide a route to challenge mistakes where appropriate.
10. **Liability.** Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Final liability wording requires Nigerian legal review.

## 3. Refunds, cancellations and complaints — draft

### Before vendor acceptance
If an order has not been accepted or preparation has not started, a cancellation request should normally be honoured and any captured payment refunded through the original payment method, subject to provider processing times.

### After preparation has started
The customer should contact support promptly. The resolution should consider the actual preparation/delivery status, the reason for cancellation, vendor evidence and applicable consumer law. Do not apply an automatic “no refunds under any circumstances” rule.

### Missing, incorrect, damaged or poor-quality items
Record the order number, affected items and a description. Request reasonable supporting information only where needed. Offer an appropriate remedy based on the facts and law, such as replacement, partial refund, full refund or another agreed resolution.

### Failed or duplicate payment
Check the payment provider's verified transaction status. Do not ask the customer to pay again until the first payment status is checked. Escalate pending or duplicate charges to the payment provider and keep a traceable case record.

### Delivery problems
If a rider cannot locate the customer, the rider should use the approved contact process and record reasonable attempts. Do not disclose a customer's full address or phone number to unrelated parties.

### Complaints process
Acknowledge complaints promptly, give a reference number, investigate with only the people who need the information, keep the customer updated and record the resolution. Escalate safety, discrimination, harassment, suspected fraud, privacy and payment complaints immediately.

Use the wording only if the app actually offers manual entry and the described location behaviour matches the implementation.

## 7. Data access, correction and deletion workflow

1. Receive the request through the published support channel.
2. Record the request date, requester, account/order reference, request type and deadline.
3. Verify identity proportionately; do not ask for passwords, card PINs or CVVs.
4. Identify relevant records across the apps, database, storage, logs and processors.
5. Check legal, accounting, payment-dispute, safety and fraud-retention requirements.
6. Export, correct, restrict or delete data as legally appropriate; delete private identity files when no longer required.
7. Ask processors to action the request where required.
8. Record what was done, what was retained and the reason.
9. Respond to the requester and explain any lawful limitation.
10. Keep a minimal audit record of the request and outcome.

Do not add a “Delete account” button that appears to work but only hides the account. Deletion must be implemented server-side, authenticated, authorised and tested.

## 11. Internal technical review — 31 October 2026 (not a legal certification)

This section records a read-only review of the connected production Supabase project and repository. It does not certify the whole platform or replace a penetration test.

### Confirmed controls
- `password_reset_challenges`, `platform_earnings` and `quickeats_admin_users` have RLS enabled, no policies, and no SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grants to either `anon` or `authenticated`. They remain inaccessible through ordinary client table APIs. Do not add broad policies merely to clear the informational linter finding.
- The inspected SECURITY DEFINER RPCs are not executable by `anon`. The admin mutation RPCs inspected call `is_admin()` or `quickeats_is_admin()` before changing admin-managed records.
- `accept_order` checks for an authenticated user, an existing rider profile, approved status, online status, an eligible unassigned order and locks the rider/order rows while accepting.
- Rider self-service profile changes are guarded by database triggers that prevent a non-admin user from changing profile role, rider identity or approval/review fields.
- Rider document submission checks required document types, duplicate types, the authenticated user's storage folder and existence in the private `rider-documents` bucket. Admin approval checks required documents have been explicitly approved.
- Paystack webhook handling verifies the signature against the raw request body and re-verifies transaction status, reference, NGN currency and amount before invoking the idempotent payment RPC.

### Remaining security actions
- **Meta webhook:** the current bot code validates `X-Hub-Signature-256` only when `META_APP_SECRET` is present. If missing, it logs a warning and continues processing unsigned webhook requests. Change to fail closed only after the correct Meta app secret has been configured and confirmed in Render; otherwise production WhatsApp order updates may stop. This setting was not changed.
- **Webhook verification token:** `VERIFY_TOKEN` currently has a sample fallback. Replace it with a unique high-entropy Render secret and remove the fallback after confirming the same value is configured in Meta. Not changed to avoid disconnecting the webhook.
- **Supabase Auth:** leaked-password protection is disabled according to the Auth advisor. The connected tool did not expose the Auth security setting, so it remains a dashboard action.
- **SECURITY DEFINER advisor:** 21 callable functions are intentionally available to signed-in clients as RPC endpoints. The inspected endpoints include ownership/admin checks; do not blanket-revoke EXECUTE or switch them to INVOKER because Rider, Vendor and Admin workflows call them. Continue reviewing any changed/new RPC before release.
- **Performance advisor:** three foreign-key indexes are missing and multiple permissive policies are reported. These are performance/maintainability findings, not proof of a data exposure. Review query plans and consolidate duplicate policies in a separate tested migration; do not remove policies without proving equivalent access remains.
- **Incident response:** define an owner, contact channel, evidence preservation, containment and legal notification decision process. Assess each suspected breach promptly under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and current NDPC guidance; do not promise a fixed notification outcome without a risk assessment.
